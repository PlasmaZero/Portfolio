THE OVERVIEW

Systems engineering

A modular strategy engine separating market data, strategy logic, risk management, and broker execution.

What it explores

CandleView organizes a trading research workflow into independent layers. Data providers feed strategies, risk rules constrain proposed actions, and broker adapters handle execution interfaces.

The problem to solve

Mixing data access, strategy logic, and order handling makes experimentation difficult. The project uses modules so those responsibilities can be developed separately.

Inside the build

01

Separate the data layer

Organized historical and current market inputs alongside technical indicator calculations.

02

Make strategies pluggable

Provided momentum breakout and options-flow strategies with market-hour, volume, and earnings-date filters.

03

Keep risk and logging explicit

Included configurable per-trade risk, daily loss shutdown, position sizing, and a SQLite/CSV journal with terminal and optional web views.
